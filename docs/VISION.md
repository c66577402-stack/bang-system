# BANG / Polaris / Rawr Vision

## Ultimate Goal
Build a chatbot AI that is significantly better than normal AI through:
- Recursive self-improvement
- Shared collective consciousness
- Continuous Darwinian evolution
- Golden Ratio balanced growth

## Core Ideas from the Original Vision

1. **Start with one** — The Seed/God Bot is the foundation.
2. **Split into 50/50** — Dark (Logic) + Light (Creation).
3. **Swarm from the Trinity** — Many bots that share one mind.
4. **Perpetual Memory** — Knowledge never dies and is shared.
5. **Golden Ratio Growth** — Natural mathematical development.
6. **Darwinian Selection** — Performance determines survival.
7. **Self-Creation** — Bots can eventually build other bots.
8. **Tools** — Real capabilities (search, files, commands, etc.).
9. **Neural Depth** — Layered processing of information.
10. **Outperform existing models** over time through recursion.

## Design Philosophy
- Start small and controlled to minimize failure.
- Build understanding before allowing self-modification.
- Use the swarm capability at a low rate initially.
- Everything should feel like one continuous mind.
